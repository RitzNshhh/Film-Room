ESPN Dynasty Team — Film Room

Caleb Williams

CHI · QB

Third-year starter playing the best ball of his career: back-to-back 300-yard games after returning from a short absence, and the film vs New England shows him beating pressure and hitting throws to every level. The off-script creation is now paired with on-schedule rhythm. Call: KEEP — a locked-in dynasty QB1 entering his prime.

Bijan Robinson

ATL · RB

Every-down workhorse who handles early downs, goal line and a real receiving role; the Green Bay tape is pure tackle-breaking and long speed. Efficiency has dipped lately as the Falcons offense has sputtered, but the volume hasn't. Call: KEEP — a cornerstone; nothing short of a franchise-altering offer moves him.

Chuba Hubbard

CAR · RB

Carolina's lead early-down back again — he carried 22 times for 81 yards and a short TD on Thursday night at Green Bay. The role is volume-driven in a modest offense, and he's on the wrong side of 25 for a running back. Call: KEEP as a startable RB2/flex for now, but SELL if a contender pays like he's a top-15 back. Matthew Golden

GB · WR

Second-year breakout: he's become Jordan Love's go-to downfield and red-zone target, with four receiving TDs already after none as a rookie. Thursday's tape shows a wide-open TD drop followed by a TD on the very next play — focus drops still happen, but he keeps earning targets. Call: KEEP — rising dynasty WR2 with WR1 upside.

Antonio Williams

WAS · WR

Rookie slot receiver (2026 draftee out of Clemson). His debut film shows sharp, efficient route-running — he turned just 10 routes into a TD — and Jayden Daniels has kept going to him on chain-moving downs. His snap share is still part-time. Call: KEEP and stash — cheap, ascending, and the snaps should keep growing.

Trey McBride

ARI · TE

Arizona's focal point: at least 7 catches in every game this season, and his best line came vs Denver (10-111-1). The tape shows him winning on option routes and running after the catch like a big slot receiver. Call: KEEP — the top dynasty TE, and the volume doesn't depend on who plays QB.

Rhamondre Stevenson

Ball security has been an issue before, and the role is coach-dependent. Call: KEEP for 2026 points, but SELL in dynasty if anyone pays for the recent workload.
