Sleeper Redraft Team — Film Room

Josh Allen

BUF · QB

MVP-level again: he scores both ways (8 rushing TDs through five games) and played clean, controlled football vs the Raiders. The Rams tape is a reminder that his rushing ceiling wins weeks on its own. Outlook: elite QB1 every week for the rest of the season.

Puka Nacua

LAR · WR

Missed the first few weeks, then came back as a target hog — 24 targets and 17 catches vs Buffalo. The tape shows him winning underneath and after the catch. Outlook: locked-in WR1 for the rest of the season while healthy.

CeeDee Lamb

DAL · WR

Dallas's alpha: a career-high 17-189-1 game vs Houston, and two TDs vs Philadelphia on Monday night. The volume and red-zone role are both intact. Outlook: WR1 for the rest of the season.

Zay Flowers

BAL · WR

Baltimore's most consistent weapon and in a breakout season — 8-137-1 with 101 yards after the catch vs Cincinnati. The film shows separation and YAC creating chunk plays on his own. Outlook: strong WR2 with weekly WR1 spikes.

Josh Jacobs

GB · RB

Back from the Commissioner's Exempt List; he's played two games. Thursday vs Carolina he got 21 carries and scored twice (a 1-yd run and a 4-yd catch), but managed only 39 rushing yards. The NFL's investigation is still open. Outlook: volume- and TD-dependent RB2 with off-field risk attached. (No qualifying game film; X coverage was off-field.)

Cam Skattebo

NYG · RB

Second-year back who went from zero touches the week before to a career-best 17-124-1 vs Houston, including a 42-yard OT run. With Tracy now on IR, the lead role should be his. He carries a hamstring tag but has a bye in Week 8. Outlook: RB2 with upside after the bye, as long as the hamstring cooperates.

Braelon Allen

NYJ · RB

With Breece Hall (quad) inactive, Allen has been the lead back — 104 rushing yards plus 6 catches vs Miami in Week 7. He was limited in practice this week, and the Jets may split work once Hall returns. Outlook: RB2 while Hall is out, then a flex/handcuff.

Matthew Golden

GB · WR

Golden's 7-79-1 on Thursday was a career high in catches. Love targets him downfield and near the goal line. The drop on a wide-open TD shows he can still lose focus at times. Outlook: rising WR2/flex the rest of the way.

Marvin Harrison Jr.

HOU · WR

New Texan via an October trade; his every-snap debut shows full-time usage right away, and he caught his first Houston TD vs the Giants. Chemistry with C.J. Stroud from Ohio State should help him climb past Arizona's dead-end usage. Outlook: WR3 with upside and improving week over week.

Jalen Coker

CAR · WR

Third-year receiver and a reliable target for Bryce Young — he opened the season with 8-138-2 and scored again Thursday on a fourth-and-3 TD. Outlook: boom/bust WR3/flex in a modest passing game.

Denzel Boston

CLE · WR

Rookie WR (2026) with a big-play role: his every-target tape vs Pittsburgh shows contested and downfield wins. QB play limits his consistency. Outlook: WR3/flex with spike weeks; keep starting him in good matchups.

Tyler Warren

IND · TE

Second-year TE who missed two games with a groin injury and is cleared to play Week 8 vs Jacksonville after full practice Friday. When healthy he's a core part of the Colts offense, including fullback-style goal-line work. Outlook: plug back in as a TE1 now that he's been cleared. (He sits in the Sleeper IR slot but isn't on the NFL's injured reserve. No qualifying film; only short single-play clips found.)

Second-year back whose role has shrunk: 13 snaps (24%) in Week 7 behind Rhamondre Stevenson after a high-ankle sprain, and he missed late-game work. The Week 2 every-carry tape showed the long speed but little created yardage behind a struggling line. Outlook: flex at best until his snaps rebound.

Brenton Strange

JAX · TE

Jacksonville's starting TE, coming off a Week 7 bye, with roughly 4–5 targets projected per game. He's a functional, low-ceiling option. Outlook: TE2 streamer; start him only in good matchups. (No qualifying game film found.)

Seahawks D/ST

SEA · D/ST

A great defensive game in Week 7: Seattle held the unbeaten Chiefs to 3 points and 213 yards, and picked off Mahomes. The front controls the run without base personnel, so the floor is high. Outlook: every-week starting D/ST.
